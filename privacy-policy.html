Privacy Policy

Application: Shafi Homes Arcade
Application ID: shafi.homes.arcade
Effective Date: November 20, 2026
Privacy Commitment: Shafi Homes Arcade is built with privacy and data minimization by design. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data with third-party advertisers. All operational data is stored securely on your local device.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Shafi Homes Arcade ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our mobile application designed for building society maintenance tracking, repair logging, automated contractor scheduling, and visitor gate pass management.

2. Information We Collect

To provide core building society management functionality, the application processes the following types of information:

  • Resident & Unit Details: Apartment/flat number, resident name, and monthly maintenance assessment records.
  • Maintenance & Repair Tickets: Issue descriptions, trade category (Plumbing, Electrical, Elevator, HVAC, Civil, Security), urgency priority, and optional photos attached to illustrate repair needs.
  • Visitor & Guest Gate Passes: Visitor name, optional contact phone number, vehicle registration number (optional), purpose of visit (Guest, Delivery, Cab, Service), and generated 4-digit security entry passcodes.
  • Financial Records: Billed maintenance amounts, sinking fund contributions, utility breakdowns, payment receipt identifiers, and marked payment status.

3. How We Use Your Information

All data collected is used solely to facilitate building society administration, including:

  • Calculating dues assessments and generating itemized payment receipts.
  • Matching repair tickets with qualified maintenance contractors and dispatching work shifts.
  • Issuing temporary security passcodes for verified visitor and delivery entry.
  • Displaying digital notices, community announcements, and facility schedules.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your society operational data is stored locally on your device using encrypted, structured SQLite database technology (Android Room). We employ industry-standard architectural safeguards to protect your records against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.

5. Payments and Financial Information

When you choose to pay maintenance dues via UPI (Unified Payments Interface), the app utilizes Android's native deep-link intent mechanism to pass transaction details (payee UPI ID, amount, reference note) to your installed, certified UPI banking applications (such as Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, or BHIM).

Shafi Homes Arcade never requests, records, or stores your banking PIN, card credentials, or bank passwords. All monetary transactions occur entirely within your selected banking institution's secure application.

6. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, lease, trade, or share your personal data with third-party marketers, advertisers, or analytics brokers.

7. Device Permissions

The application requests only minimal, necessary device permissions:

  • INTERNET: Required to enable network connectivity for UPI payment intent routing and optional Firebase cloud service integration.
  • No Sensitive Background Permissions: The app does not access your contacts, SMS, microphone, or background geographic location.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Users and management committee operators maintain full control over their data. You can delete or modify any maintenance bill, repair ticket, notice, or visitor pass directly within the application at any time. Uninstalling the app permanently removes all local database records from your device.

9. Children's Privacy

Shafi Homes Arcade is designed for adult residents, building committee members, and contractors. It is not directed to children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may periodically update our Privacy Policy. Any modifications will be posted to this page with an updated effective date. You are advised to review this policy periodically.
